# Demo walkthrough

A suggested order for showing the demo account: one student's semester, told so each step leads
to the next. About 10 minutes. Load the demo first (Account → "Load the demo account", in a
development build, signed in to the demo account); it's dated from that day.

**The story:** a second-year Earth Sciences student with two units (Mineralogy & Petrology,
Climate Systems), a coastal field trip in four days with a report to write, and a climate essay
due in about three and a half weeks.

## 1. Start here (Notes)
- The app opens on Notes with two sticky notes on the screen. Open **Start here: my semester**.
- The "Coming up" list holds live items, not text: a reminder, the field trip, a task, the exam
  and the essay deadline, each with its date. Open one.
- Point out the embedded passage ("Rocks are not permanent…"): it's live from another note.
- The Glossary chips (Lithification, Albedo, IPCC, Quartz): hover one, click one.

## 2. Thinking in structures (The rock cycle, Identifying minerals, Geological time)
- **The rock cycle:** the cycle block, the three rock types, a definition card, a pull quote, an
  unsure flag and an answered question, and "foliation" marked Clarify later (open the Clarify tool
  from the Notes tools panel to see it under To learn, the slate flag under To check). Ctrl+]
  switches a block's design (try the cycle).
- **Identifying minerals:** the identification key, the quartz vs calcite comparison, the Quartz
  profile card, maths with numbering.
- **Geological time:** the hierarchy (eon, era, period), the breakdown, half-life maths, and the
  "therefore" conclusion.
- Type `\` on an empty line to show every block on offer; `//alpha`, `-->` and `CO2` as you type.

## 3. Notes into action (Field trip: Kiama coast)
- The field trip event drawn in full at the top, the reminder, the task and its packing list.
- The horizontal timeline of the day, and the embed from the minerals notes.
- **Linked from** at the top: everything elsewhere in the app that points at this note.
- Jump to **Tasks**: the field trip report Endeavour, "waits on" tasks, sub-tasks, the checklist's
  2/8 progress, a milestone. Complete "Pack the field kit" to show the checklist reset.
- Jump to **Calendar**: the field trip, the timetable (Schedules) underneath, the tentative
  tutor consult, the study group repeating, the mid-semester break.

## 4. Weighing an argument (Essay, Week 6, Feedback loops)
- **Week 6:** the CO₂ chart, the history timeline, the energy budget, the feedback diagram,
  a concept card, Python code, an open question. The "Exam revision" tab.
- **Essay:** the debate-style argument, pros and cons on the scales, ranked options, the evidence
  pyramid, and the **Essay ideas** inbox as a board (one idea sent from the Week 6 note).
- **Feedback loops:** an idea hub collecting passages from both units, a concept and the essay.
- Notes views: **Mosaic** and **Map** (how the notes link).

## 5. Everything for one thing (Overview, Records, the rest)
- **Kinds of event and repeats:** in the Calendar, Mum's birthday says how old she turns (it's a Birthday, its first date the year she was born), and the train home shows 🚆, dimmed (Travel, shown Quiet). Press N: Event / Birthday / Travel chips at the top; pick Travel and choose ✈️ 🚆 🚗… Open the Study group (it repeats, 🔁 in the grid): one line says "Every week · from …", and **Editing: All dates ▾** chooses which dates a change goes to; nothing is copied until you change something.
- **Date calculator:** the calculator button in the Calendar header (or Quick Access → Places). Press the wand and type "the 12th Friday from 1 October 2026" or "how many days until 25 December"; Ctrl+Enter shows the answer in the Calendar.
- **Why it's warming, Weather or climate?, A case that worked** (Week 6 note): a cause-and-effect chain (hover any item to light its chain; press an item's dot, then one in the next column, to link them; Ctrl+] for the List design, again for the Fishbone), a Venn diagram (Ctrl+] for the table; edit a thing to see its set buttons) and a case study (Ctrl+] for Report, then Compact).
- **Icons:** edit a notebook (or a list, an Overview) and press its icon: emoji by group or by search, or upload a picture, which becomes a small icon you can reuse anywhere under Your icons.
- **Block guide:** Quick Access → Block guide (or "All blocks and their designs" at the foot of the \\ menu): every block with its designs and a live example; click a design to see it.
- **Converters:** Alt+= (or Quick Access → Converters). Lengths in every unit as you type; under Your converters, "CO₂ to carbon": type in either box. "+ New converter" shows the editor (formula checked as you type). In the Climate Systems lecture note, a `\convert` card works out the carbon in 2,400 kg of CO₂ with that converter; change the amount, or the converter from its dropdown.
- **AI prompt:** in the Climate Systems lecture note, after the conversion, the "Explain a climate idea" tile (a template). Click it to open: a kept section and an each-run one, `{exam}` and `{idea}` in the Fill in table. Copy puts it together with them filled in; New run (here, Use this template) puts a fresh copy below with the each-run section and fill-ins empty.
- **A number written another way:** in a note, put the cursor on a big number and press Ctrl+] a few times (69000000000 → 69,000,000,000 → 6.9×10¹⁰ → 6.9E10), or right-click it → Number format.
- **Overview:** pick the Climate essay Endeavour: every task, deadline, event and note for it.
- **The Endeavours section** (top of the nav): the tree of Endeavours, Semester 2 with its parts. Open **Semester 2**: what's behind and due this week across both assignments, a card for each; open the essay from its card; "Add to Climate essay: + Task" files the task there.
- **A semester and its subject:** on Semester 2's home, **Edit** opens the semester's own editor: its teaching dates, mid-semester break and exam period, its subjects, and "+ Subject". Open **GEOS215 Climate Systems**: its Schedule (the field excursion) and Work (the weekly quiz, worth 10%, "Best 8 of 9 count"), each one line; click one to change it. The 🪄 reads a pasted course outline into both lists.
- **Types:** Calendar → open the mid-semester exam: it's an **Exam** (a kind of Assessment), with its weight, room and allowed materials as fields. The Tasks + button lists the Types: choose **Essay** to see a form titled "New Essay", Important already on, with Plan / Draft / Proofread ticked to be made, each dated from the due date. Manage → Types shows them all; "Make this tag a Type" is on any tag's options.
- **Week by week:** open **Semester 2**: its board shows the timetable's classes, the GEOS215 lectures marked attended (click an unmarked one to mark it; unmarked ones aren't behind), and GEOS215's board shows its Work: the reading the day before each lecture and the Friday quiz. One reading was missed, so it's Behind; click it to tick it done. Right-click a date to skip it or open it, where its own details (pages, a mark) go on that date alone. GEOS215's Work due soon also shows above the task list.
- **Goals:** Semester 2's home opens with its goals: a distinction (checked in by hand, 71 against 75: "+ Check in" 78 and it offers to mark it achieved), keeping up the readings (the reading Work done), 10 hours of study a week (from the Study hours tracker). The climate essay's goal counts its tasks. The Endeavours page shows each one's goals as a figure.
- **Templates:** Endeavours page → the + (or From a template…) → University subject. Click the 🪄 and paste this (one a line), then press Enter:
  `GEOS301 Earth Resources` / `Lectures: Monday 10-12, Wednesday 1-2pm, Earth Sciences LT1` / `Tutorial: Thursday 2–3pm` / `Lab 1, week 4 Tuesday, 12-13` / `Weekly readings` / `Field report, 30%, due 21 November` / `Mid-semester exam, Week 7, 25%`. The lists fill in: two lecture rows, a tutorial, a lab, a reading before each lecture, two assessments. Add an assessment "Lab report 1", When "After…", 2 weeks, from Lab 1, and watch "This will make" date it. Set it up, look at its home, then Undo from the toast.
- **Restructure:** on Semester 2's home → Restructure… → Copy its shape (a Semester 3, empty but set up the same); or on the climate essay, Split off a part (choose a few tasks). Each has Undo.
- **Endeavours inside Endeavours:** choose **Semester 2** in the header's Endeavour filter: the field trip report, the essay and Uni admin all show, since each is part of it. Edit Semester 2 (Manage) to see what it contains; edit the essay to see "Part of: Semester 2" (it could be part of more than one).
- **Tasks linked to an event:** open the mid-semester exam in the Calendar. Its Tasks show the revision (preparation, due when the exam starts) and the feedback follow-up. Move the exam's date: the revision moves with it, with Undo in the toast. On an Overview's Runway the two sit under the exam.
  Then the saved "Due in the next fortnight".
- **Records:** Study hours and Field samples trackers, the Morning revision routine.
- **Lists:** the Dictionary (words are highlighted in notes), the reading list.
- **Quick Access** (Q): the pins (Start here, the essay, GEOS215, the field trip report, the
  reading list, the morning routine, Review) and the Places row. Right-click any note, notebook or
  list → Pin to Quick Access, then Q shows it. **Go to** (Ctrl+G, or Alt+G from the Quick Access
  tab): search, recent notes. **Glossary** and **Review** (Important passages due for review).
- **Scratchpad** (Alt+P from anywhere, or its pin in Quick Access): a few things jotted down; Copy
  all / Cut all, and the paper or simple look. It's also the second pinned notebook in Notes.
- **New calendar item** (Calendar, N): the 🪄 beside the title, then paste "Every 3rd Sunday of
  the month until December 2027 hash run at Minervastrasse 65, Zürich https://…" and every field
  fills in. Ctrl+Tab switches Event / Reminder / Deadline.
- Fitness and Portfolio, briefly, if they're part of the pitch.
